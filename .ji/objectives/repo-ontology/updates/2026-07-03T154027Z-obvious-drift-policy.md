# Obvious drift policy rebaseline

Updated repo guidance and this Objective's standing orientation to prefer fixing obvious domain-documentation drift instead of merely reporting it.

New rule: if adjacent work exposes stale vocabulary, paths, counts, package names, or `Avoid` aliases that are source-backed, small/local, and require no new terminology or product decision, fix the drift inline. If the drift is broad, ambiguous, or decision-bearing, record/report it and keep it as a focused follow-up rather than silently widening the slice.

Touched guidance:

- `AGENTS.md` Context and routing rule.
- `.ji/objectives/repo-ontology/orientation.md` active-agent orientation.
- `.ji/objectives/repo-ontology/objective.md` non-goals, progress criteria, and drift-risk mitigation.
- `.ji/objectives/repo-ontology/roadmap.md` standing operating direction.
