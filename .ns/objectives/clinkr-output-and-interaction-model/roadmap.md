## Parked

- A unified Request/Response/Progress/Notice/Elicitation ontology.
- `ClinkrResponse`, `ClinkrFinalPresentation`, or a general event stream absent a concrete consumer that cannot use invocation-scoped output capture.
- Arbitrary rich terminal libraries inside Pi; use Pi runtime extension UI capabilities instead.
- PTY, raw-mode, key-event, full-screen, cursor, resize, and terminal-session virtualization.
- Third-host generalization, including MCP, until a concrete production consumer exists.
- Comparative framework research unless a specific implementation decision cannot be made from current consumers and adapters.
- Repository-wide migration or deletion of unrelated legacy Clinkr APIs.
