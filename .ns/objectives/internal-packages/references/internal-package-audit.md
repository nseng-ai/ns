## Accepted follow-ups (candidate future roadmap rows)

Promote/park items warranting future roadmap work, in rough priority order:

1. **Topology-report script suite → `packages/internal/*`.** ~100 KB of untested `.mjs` (`skills/architecture-topology-report/scripts/*`) is the strongest concrete promotion candidate after the first resident. Sequence behind `typescript-style-guard`. → parked roadmap row.

3. **`@ns/review-reinvention-scanner` reuse watch.** No action now — it already carries a promotion path as the canonical consumer-first prototype. Revisit only on second-review reuse or review graduation. Covered by the existing Parked "Migrations of audit candidates beyond the first resident" row; not separately parked.
4. **`clean-stale-node-modules-leftovers.mjs`.** Low priority; revisit only if it needs tests or gains a sibling script. Not separately parked.
