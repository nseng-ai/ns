# ji naming brief

The naming story and brand rationale for **ji**, the product formerly named SDL ("Source
Development Lifecycle"). The binding verdicts — the casing rule, the accepted collisions,
the npm scope plan — live in [ADR 0024](adr/0024-rename-sdl-to-ji.md); this brief is the
deliberation and the narrative, and it is the only in-repo home for the marketing story.

## The spine

**Engineers are not factory managers; they are sorcerers.**

The prevailing industry story casts the engineer as a manager of AI workers — fleets,
throughput, supervision dashboards. ji's story is the opposite: the engineer is a caster,
and the power is in knowing what to invoke and how to phrase it. The djinn, the
spellbook, and the jib are flavor orbiting that one sentence — not a mapped ontology.
Resist building a web of terms and analogies out of them.

## How the name happened

**jib** was the original candidate. Then **ji** appeared and displaced it on sight. Three
associations converged:

- **Loop variables.** `j` and `i` are the two canonical index names, fused into one word.
- **The djinn homage.** A summoned power that does the work.
- **Two-letter CLIs are sweet.** `uv` and `ty` as prior art.

Also considered and rejected:

- **sdl** (keeping the status quo) — an acronym that has to be expanded to mean anything,
  and whose letters already belong to other well-known projects.
- **asdl** — tried to modernize sdl but inherited its acronym problem, and collides with
  Python's ASDL.
- **erk** — sounds like "irk"; unpleasant to say aloud.

## Usage

- Pronounced **"jee"**.
- Always lowercase: `ji`, never `JI` or `Ji` — including at the start of a sentence;
  rewrite the sentence instead.
- No expansion. It does not stand for anything.
